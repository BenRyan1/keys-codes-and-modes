For Schools: Student Privacy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

This page is for teachers, principals, and district technology and privacy reviewers. It explains exactly what Music Theory Pro does and does not collect when students use it in class.

The Short Version

  • No account. Students open the link and start. No login, no sign-up, no email, no name.
  • No student data collected. Music Theory Pro does not ask for, receive, or store personal information from users of any age.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads. The app sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and shows no advertising.
  • Microphone stays local. The optional "listen" feature analyzes sound inside the browser. Nothing is recorded or sent to us.
  • AI features are off. No student input is sent to any AI service.

What Stays on the Student's Device

Music Theory Pro saves a few settings in the browser's own local storage so the app remembers them next time: game high scores, sound effect on/off, the chosen microphone, and whether a panel is open. This information never leaves the device and is never sent to us. Clearing the browser's site data erases it.

Outside Services the App Loads From

Like most websites, the app loads some files from other servers. When a browser requests a file, that server receives standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type). We do not receive or use this information.

Student Privacy Laws

FERPA. Music Theory Pro does not create, receive, or maintain education records or any personally identifiable information from students, so no student records are held by Keys, Codes & Modes.

SOPIPA and AB 1584 (California). We do not use student information for targeted advertising, do not build profiles of students, and do not sell or disclose student information. Music Theory Pro collects none to begin with.

COPPA. Because Music Theory Pro collects no personal information from children, it may be used by students under 13.

Data privacy agreements. We will sign the California Student Data Privacy Agreement on request and will complete district privacy and security questionnaires.

What This Page Covers

This page covers Music Theory Pro at mtp.keyscodesandmodes.com. The KCM Console and paid apps ask for an email address to sign up. They are intended for teachers and adults, not student accounts. Our general Privacy Policy covers those services.

Using It in Class

  • Open mtp.keyscodesandmodes.com in a current web browser. Nothing to install.
  • Works on a projector or interactive board for whole-class teaching, and on student laptops or tablets.
  • Free to use. A free in-class demonstration is available for Santa Barbara area schools.

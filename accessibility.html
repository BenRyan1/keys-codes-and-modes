Accessibility Statement

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Keys, Codes & Modes is built so that more people can understand music. That includes people who use a keyboard instead of a mouse, screen readers, zoom, or reduced-motion settings. This statement covers keyscodesandmodes.com, console.keyscodesandmodes.com, and mtp.keyscodesandmodes.com.

Our Goal

We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, the standard used by U.S. schools and public agencies.

What We Have Done

  • Automated accessibility testing of our main pages and apps, with fixes for the issues found
  • Text color contrast raised to meet WCAG AA on tested pages
  • Labels on buttons and controls for screen readers
  • Keyboard access to interactive panels, plus "skip" links on key pages
  • Support for your device's reduced-motion setting on key pages
  • Pages can be zoomed and resized

Known Limitations

KCM teaches music through color, shape, and sound, so some tools depend on seeing color or hearing audio. Where we can, colors are paired with note names and labels. Some highly visual tools (for example, animated diagrams and 3D visualizers) may not yet work fully with screen readers. We are improving these over time.

Report a Problem

If anything on our sites is hard to use, please tell us. Include the page address and what happened, and we will work with you on a fix or another way to get the same learning.
